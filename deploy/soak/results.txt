RESULT: FAILED on session3 (4.61 MB/h, limit 2.0). The gateways and session1 are flat; session2 flat but close (1.62).

Why (diagnosed, not yet fixed):
- Growth tracks the active boards each instance owns: the 4 soak boards (~5,500 ops each by the end)
  were owned 3 by session3, 1 by session2, 0 by session1. About 1.5 MB/h per active board, ~1 KB per op.
- Heap histogram (session3, minute ~95): dev.concord.crdt.Lww$Register 12,016 and Hlc 8,878 objects plus
  their Strings/HashMap nodes: CRDT state. On one soak board 1,175 of 1,228 added cards were deleted;
  their tombstones and registers are kept for the board's life (SPEC decision 9), and each board keeps its
  last 10,000 ACKed op_ids (SPEC 4.3), which was still filling.
- The SPEC has no rule for unloading a board nobody has open: a loaded board stays in memory until its
  instance stops or hands it over. So heap grows with the history of every board loaded, unbounded.
- The soak workload adds and deletes cards without end, so it grows board state by design; it cannot be
  flat on a JVM that owns an active board even without a leak.

Errors: 4, all at minutes 26-31 (08:48-08:54 UTC): Postgres stalled (checkpoints of 62s and 54s) while
rehearsal images were being built on the same laptop; JDBC socketTimeout (10s) fired, gateways answered
8 requests 503 "storage unavailable", 16 commits failed and every one was reconciled. No data lost.

Proposed (awaiting approval): amendment 26, an owner releases a board after 10 minutes with no
subscribers; and a rerun on an idle machine with a fixed-size workload (field edits, moves, presence on
a fixed set of cards) so the criterion tests for leaks rather than board growth.

  - each op costs ~163 B of heap until the board's ACK cache holds 10,000 (SPEC 4.3): at most ~1.6 MB per board, then 0;
  - editing existing cards (titles, descriptions, labels, moves) costs ~0 once the cache is full;
  - each deleted card keeps ~3.0 KB of heap and 534 B of snapshot for the board's life (tombstone + registers, decision 9).
projection: a board used daily for 30 days, assuming 500 ops a day of which 50 delete a card: 15,000 ops, 1,500 deleted cards -> heap while loaded 6.2 MB (ACK cache 1.6 MB, capped; deleted cards 4.5 MB), snapshot +0.80 MB. Live cards and edits to them add ~0. With amendment 26 only boards someone has open count against a Session instance's 384 MB heap.
